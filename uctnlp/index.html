University of Cape Town

UCT NLP Group

We do research in natural language processing — with a focus on developing language resources and language models for low-resource South African and African languages.

Research Topics

LLMs for low-resource languages

  • Modelling, translation, and generation where data is scarce

Linguistic structure in text

  • Segmentation, parsing, and meaning representation

South African language data

  • Building NLP datasets and models for multiple languages

Text generation

  • Faithful, controllable generation and evaluation
