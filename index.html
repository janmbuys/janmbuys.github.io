About

I am a Senior Lecturer (≈ Assistant Professor) in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Cape Town. My research area is Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning. My current research focuses on text generation, linguistic structure prediction, and low-resource language processing.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Washington, working with Yejin Choi. I completed my PhD at the University of Oxford, supervised by Phil Blunsom. Before that I obtained Masters and undergraduate degrees in Computer Science at the University of Stellenbosch in South Africa.
